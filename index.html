Chicken

Chicken Adobo

The iconic savory and tangy Filipino national dish simmered in soy sauce, vinegar, and garlic.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg chicken pieces (thighs/drumsticks)
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 1/2 cup cane vinegar
  • 1 head garlic, crushed
  • 1 tsp whole black peppercorns
  • 3 dried bay leaves
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 2 tbsp cooking oil

Chicken Curry

A rich, creamy Filipino-style curry cooked with coconut milk, curry powder, and vibrant vegetables.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg chicken cut into serving pieces
  • 1.5 cups coconut milk
  • 2 tbsp curry powder
  • 1 large potato, cubed
  • 1 large carrot, cubed
  • 1 red bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 onion & 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp fish sauce (patis)
  • 2 tbsp cooking oil

Sinigang na Baboy

Sinigang na Baboy

A comforting Filipino sour soup made with tender pork ribs, tamarind broth, and fresh vegetables.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg pork belly or ribs, cut into chunks
  • 1 pack (40g) tamarind soup base mix
  • 1 bunch kangkong (water spinach)
  • 1 cup radish (labanos), sliced
  • 1 cup taro root (gabi), cubed
  • 1 medium onion & 2 tomatoes, quartered
  • 2 pieces green long peppers (siling haba)
  • 6–8 cups water
  • Fish sauce to taste

Seafood

Buttered Shrimp

Juicy fresh shrimp tossed in garlic butter sauce and lemon soda for a sweet and savory taste.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg fresh shrimp, cleaned and whiskers trimmed
  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter
  • 1 head garlic, finely minced
  • 1/2 cup lemon-lime soda (e.g., Sprite/7Up)
  • 1 tbsp oyster sauce
  • Salt & ground black pepper to taste
  • Chopped parsley or spring onions for garnish

Pork

Pork Sisig

A sizzling, savory, and crunchy Kapampangan masterpiece made from chopped pork, onions, and chili peppers.

Ingredients

  • 500g pork belly & 300g pig ears (boiled and grilled)
  • 2 large onions, finely chopped
  • 3-4 bird's eye chilies (siling labuyo), chopped
  • 1/4 cup calamansi juice
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise or pork liver spread
  • Salt, pepper, and soy sauce to taste
  • 1 raw egg (optional, for sizzling plate)

Beef

Beef Caldereta

A hearty, rich, and slightly spicy tomato-based beef stew loaded with potatoes, carrots, bell peppers, and liver spread.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg beef chuck, cut into cubes
  • 1 cup tomato sauce & 1/2 cup tomato paste
  • 1/2 cup liver spread
  • 2 large potatoes & 2 carrots, cubed
  • 1 green and 1 red bell pepper, sliced
  • 1/2 cup green peas
  • 1 onion & 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 cups beef broth or water
  • 2 tbsp cooking oil & chili flakes (optional)
